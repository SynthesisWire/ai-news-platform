# Product Definition

## Positioning

**AI Research Intelligence** rather than generic AI news.

Core promise:

> Understand not only what happened in AI, but what it means, how strong the evidence is, how it connects to prior work, and where the trend is going.

## Public content primitives

1. **Daily Brief** — editorial synthesis of the day.
2. **Research Item / Story** — atomic development with claim/evidence/limitations.
3. **Topic / Trend** — longitudinal thesis with momentum and supporting/contradicting evidence.
4. **Research Note** — a cross-item hypothesis, contradiction, or correlation.
5. **Concept** — glossary/technical explanation such as KV-cache, MoE, OPD, POMDP.
6. **Entity** — model, company, paper, benchmark, organization, person, dataset.

## Editorial contract

Every public Research Item should distinguish:

- what is claimed;
- what was demonstrated;
- evidence strength;
- limitations;
- generalizability;
- why it matters;
- primary source(s);
- vendor evidence vs independent evidence;
- corrections/version history.

## Languages

Initial: `pt-BR` and `en`.

Translations are renderings of one canonical semantic record. Numeric evidence, citations, entities, benchmark names, model names, and relationships are not independently regenerated per language.

## Distribution

Website is canonical. Projections:

- RSS / Atom;
- topic-specific feeds;
- X posts / threads;
- newsletter;
- future public API;
- future custom user feeds and alerts.
