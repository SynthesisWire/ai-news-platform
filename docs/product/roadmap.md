# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation

- Domain model and database schema.
- Source ingestion pipeline.
- Deduplication + canonicalization.
- Evidence/claim extraction pipeline.
- Editorial status workflow.
- Notion import/sync adapter for the existing AI Research Hub.
- Public read API.

## Phase 1 — Public MVP

- PT-BR + English website.
- Daily Brief pages.
- Research Item pages.
- Topic/Trend pages.
- Concepts/glossary.
- Search: keyword + semantic hybrid.
- RSS/Atom feeds.
- SEO, sitemap, structured metadata.
- Corrections/version history.

## Phase 2 — Distribution

- X publishing workflow with human gate.
- Newsletter projection.
- Topic-specific feeds.
- OpenGraph/social cards.
- Scheduled publishing and publication audit trail.

## Phase 3 — Research Intelligence

- Typed relationship graph views.
- Evidence-backed Q&A over published knowledge.
- Timeline/trend visualizations.
- Supporting vs contradicting evidence.
- Watchlists and alerts.

## Phase 4 — Platform

- Accounts, saved topics, custom feeds.
- Public/partner API.
- Premium research workspaces.
- Organization-specific private research overlays.
